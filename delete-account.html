Delete your account

Last updated 2026-09-27

You can delete your Forma account and all of its data at any time, in the app or by email.

In the app

Open Forma, tap the gear icon on the Profile tab to open Settings, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account. Type “Delete” and tap Delete.

If our server can’t be reached at that moment, the app still deletes everything on your phone and tells you the account itself could not be removed. Email us and we’ll finish it.

What is deleted

Your account and sign-in details; your username and profile photo; your plans, routines, workout history, workout photos and ranks; your body-scan photos and scores; the people you follow, your reactions and comments, and the people you blocked and the reports you sent. Everything the app stored on your iPhone is removed too, including your sign-in.

This can’t be undone. If you want a copy first, use Settings → Export My Data before you delete. Copies in our hosting provider’s routine backups expire within 30 days.

Without the app

Email muneebrafishakoor@gmail.com from the address you signed up with — or, for a guest account, tell us your username — and ask us to delete your account. We’ll confirm once it’s done, within 30 days.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account doesn’t cancel an App Store subscription. Cancel it in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
