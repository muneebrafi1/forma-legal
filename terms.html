The service

Forma helps you plan and log strength training: training plans, workout logging, muscle-group ranks, a body scan and following friends. Some features need an internet connection, an account or a subscription. We may improve, change or remove features over time.

Not medical advice

Forma gives general fitness information, training plans and estimates. It isn’t medical advice and it isn’t a substitute for a doctor, physiotherapist or qualified coach. Talk to a medical professional before you start a new training programme, especially if you have an injury or a medical condition, are pregnant, or haven’t trained in a while.

Train within your limits, use good form and suitable equipment, and stop if you feel pain, dizziness or shortness of breath. You train at your own risk.

Ranks, strength predictions, plan suggestions and body-scan scores are estimates made from the numbers and photos you provide. They are not measurements of your body or your health.

Tracking workouts is free. Forma Pro is an auto-renewing subscription that unlocks more: the AI physique scan and its scores, and every plan in the plan library. It costs $49.99 per year or $14.99 per month. For a few minutes after setup, and again when you finish the Starter goals, you may be offered the yearly plan at $34.99 per year. There is no free trial.

Prices are in US dollars; Apple charges the equivalent in your local currency and may add tax. The price and billing period are shown on Apple’s purchase sheet before you confirm.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. The subscription renews automatically for the same period and price unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; your account is charged for the renewal within the 24 hours before the period ends.

You can manage or cancel your subscription in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal, and you keep Pro until the end of the period you have paid for. Deleting the app or your account doesn’t cancel a subscription.
