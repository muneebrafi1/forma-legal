Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-09-27

Forma is a strength-training app for iPhone. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes and the choices you have. “We” means the developer of Forma. “You” means the person using the app.

Short version: we keep what the app needs to work — your account, your training and the photos you choose to add. We don’t sell your data, we don’t show ads, there are no analytics or tracking tools in the app, and we don’t track you across other apps or websites.

Your account

You can sign up with an email address and password, with Sign in with Apple, or continue as a guest. Accounts are run by Supabase, our database and hosting provider, in its Asia Pacific (Mumbai, India) region.

For an email account we store your email address, your password in hashed form, and your username. With Apple we receive your email address (Apple may give us a private relay address instead) and the basic profile details Apple shares, such as your name. A guest account has no email at all, only a random ID.

Supabase sends the account emails the app needs, such as confirming your address or resetting your password.

Setup questions

When you set up the app we ask about your goal, training experience, body weight, biological sex, how often you train, your equipment, workout length, any injuries and how you heard about us. These answers stay on your iPhone. They are used on the phone to build your training plan and estimate your ranks, and they are not sent to our servers.

Your training

Your plans and routines, the workouts you finish (name, date, duration, exercises, sets, weights and reps, total volume and any description you add) and your muscle-group ranks are saved on your iPhone. When you’re signed in they are also backed up to your account, so they survive a new phone or a reinstall. Custom exercises you create stay on your iPhone.

Friends and what others can see

Other people signed in to Forma can find you by username and follow you. Following doesn’t need your approval.

People who follow you can see the workouts you finish — name, date, duration, sets, volume, exercises, description and any workout photo — and can react and comment. Your username, profile photo and ranks, who you follow, and the reactions and comments you leave are visible to other signed-in users.

Treat anything in your workout feed as visible to other people using the app. If you don’t want a workout shown, delete it from your history; its photo is deleted with it.

You can remove someone from your followers, and block anyone from the “…” menu beside their name or comment. A blocked person’s workouts, comments and reactions disappear from your app, and Friends → Blocked accounts lets you undo it. If you report a comment or a person, we keep the report so we can review it: who sent it, who and what it is about, the reason, any note you add and, for a comment, its text. Your blocks and reports are stored with your account and deleted with it.

Photos

Adding a photo is always your choice. The app asks for access to your camera or photo library only when you take or pick one, and it only reads the photo you choose. Before any photo leaves your phone, the app shrinks it to at most 1024 pixels on its longest side.

Profile photos are stored in a public folder: anyone who has a photo’s web address can open it, so don’t use a picture you want to keep private. Workout photos are stored in a private folder that only you and the people who follow you can open.

Body scan

The body scan is optional. When you start a scan, the photo you chose is sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which passes it to OpenAI through OpenAI’s API to estimate seven scores. Our server doesn’t keep the photo; it returns the scores and discards it. To apply a limit of 5 scans a day, it records only the time of each scan against your account, and deletes that record after two days. Under OpenAI’s API terms, data sent this way isn’t used to train their models, and OpenAI may keep it for up to 30 days to monitor for abuse before deleting it.

If you’re signed in, the app also saves the scan photo and its scores to your account so you can track your progress. Scan photos are stored in a private folder that only you can open — not the people who follow you, and not other users. They are kept until you delete them: on the Body scan tab, tap Delete this scan, which removes the photo and scores from your iPhone and every scan saved to your account. Deleting your account removes them too.

Scan scores are visual estimates from one photo. They are not medical or body-composition measurements.

Subscriptions

Purchases are made and billed by Apple. We never see your card or payment details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives your App Store purchase and subscription details, an ID linked to your account (not your name or email) and basic technical details such as the app version and your App Store country.

Notifications

Workout reminders and rest-timer alerts are scheduled on your iPhone itself. They don’t use a push token and nothing about them is sent to us. Turn reminders off in the app’s Settings under Workout reminders, or turn off notifications for the app in iPhone Settings.

No ads, analytics or tracking

The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools, and we don’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

When the app checks for updates it contacts Expo’s update service, which receives technical details such as the app version and a random installation ID — not your account or your training. Like any internet service, our providers see your IP address when the app connects to them and keep it in short-lived server logs.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers that run the app, and only what each one needs: Supabase (accounts, database, file storage and our server code), OpenAI (body-scan analysis, only when you start a scan), RevenueCat (subscription status), Apple (App Store purchases and Sign in with Apple) and Expo (app updates).

We don’t sell or rent your information, and we don’t share it with anyone else unless the law requires it.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (TLS) and is encrypted at rest by our provider. Every table in our database has access rules, so an account can read only what this policy says it can.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. Deleting a workout removes it and its photo from your account. When you delete your account we delete it immediately, together with everything linked to it; copies in our provider’s routine backups expire within 30 days. Deleting the app removes what it stored on your iPhone except your sign-in, which iOS keeps in its secure Keychain, so reinstalling signs you back in. To remove that too, tap Sign out in Settings before you delete the app.

Export or delete your data

To download your data, open Settings (the account circle at the top right of Train or Profile) and tap Export my data. You get a file with your profile, plans, routines, custom exercises, workout history and ranks that you can save or send anywhere. Photos aren’t included; email us if you want copies of them.

To delete your account, open Settings and tap Delete account at the bottom, type “Delete” and confirm. This deletes your account and everything backed up with it — workouts, plans, routines, ranks, follows, reactions, comments, blocks, reports you sent, profile photo, workout photos and body scans — and everything the app stored on your iPhone. It can’t be undone. You can also ask us by email.

Where it’s processed

Our database is in India. OpenAI, RevenueCat and Expo process data in the United States, and our providers may use other countries too. Where the law requires it, we rely on safeguards such as the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

We use your information to provide the app you asked for. The body-scan photo is processed only because you choose to start a scan, and you can stop using the feature at any time.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or California law), you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your information, or object to how we use it. You can export your data and delete your account yourself in the app. For anything else, email muneebrafishakoor@gmail.com and we’ll reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Forma isn’t meant for anyone under 13, or under 16 in the European Economic Area. We don’t knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we’ll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we’ll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we’ll tell you in the app before it applies.
