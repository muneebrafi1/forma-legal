Forma support

Last updated 2026-09-27

Help with Forma, the strength-training app for iPhone.

Get help

Email muneebrafishakoor@gmail.com. Tell us your iPhone model, your iOS version and what you were doing when something went wrong, and we’ll get back to you.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. To cancel or change your plan, open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. To restore a purchase on a new phone, open Settings in the app (the gear icon on the Profile tab) → Your Subscription → Restore past purchases. For a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

Guest accounts

A guest account lives only on your phone until you save it. To keep your training if you change phone, open Settings → Account Information → Save my account and add an email and password. Nothing you have logged is lost.

Workout reminders

Turn the daily reminder on or off in the app’s Settings under Workout reminders. If the switch won’t stay on, allow notifications for the app in iPhone Settings.

Your data

Download a copy of your data from Settings → Export My Data. You can delete your account and all of its data at any time from Settings → Delete Account; the delete-account page explains exactly what is removed. The privacy policy explains what the app collects and why.
