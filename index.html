Forma support

Last updated 2026-10-02

Help with Forma, the strength-training app for iPhone.

Get help

Email muneebrafishakoor@gmail.com. Tell us your iPhone model, your iOS version and what you were doing when something went wrong, and we’ll get back to you.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. To cancel or change your plan, open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. To restore a purchase on a new phone, open Settings in the app (the account circle at the top right of Train or Profile) → Your subscription → Restore past purchases. For a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

Guest accounts

A guest account is backed up like any other, but until you save it, it can only be reached from this phone: it has no email or password to sign back in with. To keep your training if you change phone, open Settings → Account information → Save my account and add an email and password. Nothing you have logged is lost.

Workout reminders

A reminder comes on your training days only: the training days you picked, or your plan’s days spread through the week (with no plan and no days picked, it comes each day). It skips a day you have already trained. Turn reminders on or off, and pick their time, in the app’s Settings under Workout reminders. If notifications are turned off for the app in iPhone Settings, no reminder can appear until you turn them back on there.

Report or block someone

Tap the “…” beside a comment, a workout or a person and choose Report or Block. Friends → Blocked accounts lists everyone you have blocked and lets you undo it. We read reports every day and act on them within 24 hours.

Your data

Download a copy of your data from Settings → Export my data. You can delete your account and all of its data at any time from Settings → Delete account; the delete-account page explains exactly what is removed. The privacy policy explains what the app collects and why.
